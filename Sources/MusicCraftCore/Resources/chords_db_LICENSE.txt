Guitar chord voicings — chords-db
==================================

`chords_db_guitar.json` in this directory is `lib/guitar.json` from the
chords-db project, vendored VERBATIM (byte-for-byte) as the source of all
guitar-voicing data in MusicCraftCore's VoicingLibrary.

Source:   https://github.com/tombatossals/chords-db  (lib/guitar.json)
Vendored: 2026-06-17 (master branch)
Coverage: 12 roots × 86 qualities, 828 chord entries, 3283 fingering positions,
          including 41 slash/inversion voicings (the bass note is encoded in the
          suffix string, e.g. "m/C", "/E"). Standard tuning only.

This replaces the former `guitar_voicings.json` ("legacy Cantus curated subset",
~2-3 root-position voicings per chord across 6 qualities, no inversions, no
stated license). chords-db is permissively licensed (MIT) and far richer.

The file is kept byte-identical to upstream so the provenance is verifiable and
re-import is trivial. MusicCraftCore decodes its native shape at runtime
(see VoicingLibrary.swift) and does not modify it.
